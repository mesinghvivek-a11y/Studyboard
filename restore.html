Study Board — data recovery

Finds targets whose allocated days were lost and puts them back from your own backups. It only ever touches your own account.
Before you start: close Study Board everywhere (the Android app, other browser tabs, other devices) and make sure the fixed version of the app is live. Otherwise the next time the app opens it can undo the restore.
